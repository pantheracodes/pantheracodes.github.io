Strategic Venture Dossier • Founding Atelier: San Francisco

MAISON ANGLAISE

A zero-capital, subversive luxury house abstracting the human body into architecture, bilingual code, and Pacific Rim couture.

01. Executive Thesis & Origin

Brand Manifesto

Born in San Francisco from the lived resilience of a Venezuelan trans muse and a San Francisco State University mathematics student, Maison Anglaise abstracts the human body into pure geometry, coded language, and subversive luxury fashion.

To the outside observer, the house projects old-world European couture prestige. To insiders across Spanish, French, English, and German cultures, its three sub-lines—Mise Anglaise, Deuce Anglaise, and Süsse Anglaise—encode a witty linguistic progression that transforms physical anatomy into sculptural tailoring, negative space, and brand identity.

03. Zero-Capital San Francisco Launch Roadmap

Execution Strategy
Phase I • Months 1–2 ($0 Upfront)

Digital Flagship & Editorial Storytelling

  • Deploy the multilingual web storefront on free static hosting (GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages) anchored in San Francisco.
  • Launch @maisonanglaise visual lookbooks, mathematical pattern-making sketches, and bilingual cultural commentary across Instagram and TikTok.
  • Build an early-access VIP waitlist prior to producing any physical inventory.
Phase II • Months 3–4 ($0 Inventory Risk)

Custom-Labeled On-Demand Capsule (Mise Anglaise)

  • Partner with custom-label print-on-demand ateliers (Apliiq / Printful) to sew woven Mise Anglaise waistband and neck labels onto heavyweight tees and sculpted bodysuits.
  • Customers pay upfront at retail ($65–$95); supplier fulfills and ships directly, generating $35–$55 net margin per unit with zero unsold stock.
Phase III • Months 5–8 (Customer-Funded Production)

7-Day Pre-Order Drops & SF Garment District Micro-Batches

  • Release limited 7-day pre-orders for Deuce Anglaise and Süsse Anglaise signature pieces, collecting 100% of revenue before cutting fabric.
  • Contract local San Francisco sample makers (SoMa / Mission District) for exact-count micro-batches, followed by private trunk shows in SF, Los Angeles, and Tokyo.

05. Governance, Equity & Compliance

Legal Structure
Protecting Co-Founders: Formalize the entity in California only once initial pre-order revenue validates demand. Under U.S. tax law, an undocumented co-founder may legally hold passive ownership equity in a California LLC using an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN) and receive profit distributions, while active U.S. W-2 payroll employment must wait until valid Employment Authorization (EAD) is secured.
